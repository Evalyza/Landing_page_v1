Request my diagnosis

Who is it for?

For teams of 15 to 60 people that have grown in the last year: more sales reps, more customer success, more customers. Right when what used to work by word of mouth starts to break and the founding team can no longer see everything first-hand.

It's not for you if…

you have very few customers with bespoke projects: there isn't enough activity yet to draw conclusions.

How the diagnosis works

  1. 01

    We listen

    Interviews by chat or voice with 3-5 people on the team, whenever suits you.

  2. 02

    We organise

    Each piece of evidence goes into one of the framework's 8 areas, with its source.

  3. 03

    We score

    What's defined, how it's run, whether it's measured and whether it will hold up as you keep growing.

  4. 04

    We recommend

    A plan prioritised by impact and effort, explained in a 60-minute session.
