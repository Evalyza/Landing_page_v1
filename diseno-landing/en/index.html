How your agents work

Four specialised agents go through your company the way a great operations consultant would. You only see the result.

Listen

Interviews your team by chat or voice and reads the documents you choose to share: processes, OKRs, minutes, boards.

Sort

Every piece of evidence is mapped to an area of the framework. Nothing is a loose opinion: everything has a source.

Score

Assesses each process on four dimensions: what is defined, how it is run, whether it is measured and whether it will hold as you grow.

Recommend

Delivers a plan prioritised by impact and effort, with suggested owners and the reason behind each step.

        Frequently asked questions

        What do I need to get started?

        An initial session to configure the agents and access to the sources you want to include. They take care of the interviews and the reading.

        Who sees my company's data?

        Only the people you authorise. You choose which documents, tools and people are part of the diagnosis.

        How is it different from consulting?

        The agents work in parallel and keep a trail for every conclusion, so the diagnosis can be repeated every quarter to measure progress.

        How much will it cost?

        We haven't published pricing yet. People on the waitlist will see it first.

        Does it work for very small teams?

        Yes. The framework adapts to your stage, so for a small team it focuses on what will help you grow without breaking.

        Can I talk to someone before signing up?

        Of course. Leave your number and our customer information team will call you. Book a call

        Does Ozmetra certify my company in ISO?

        No. We help you prepare the processes, documentation and evidence that standards such as ISO 9001 or ISO/IEC 20000-1 require. Certification is granted by an accredited body after its audit.

        What is your method based on?

        On the LightSME and LightStartup frameworks, published in a scientific journal and conference, based on the ISO/IEC 33000 family and validated with real SME and startup cases.
