How the agents work

Four specialised agents go through each startup the way a good operations analyst would. Your team only sees the result.

Listen

Interviews the startup's team by chat or voice and reads the documents it chooses to share: processes, OKRs, minutes, boards.

Sort

Every piece of evidence is mapped to an area of the framework. Nothing is a loose opinion: everything has a source.

Score

Assesses each process on four dimensions: what is defined, how it is run, whether it is measured and whether it will hold as you grow.

Recommend

Delivers a plan prioritised by impact and effort, with the reason for each step: the basis for mentoring.

        Frequently asked questions

        How does the pilot work?

        You try it at no cost with your next intake. We set up the agents with your selection criteria, the startups share the information they choose and your team sees the results. In return, you help us improve the product with your feedback.

        Who sees each startup's data?

        Each startup decides which documents, tools and people take part and what it shares with the programme. Only the people you authorise have access.

        Does it replace the selection committee?

        No. Ozmetra provides evidence on how each candidate works on the inside, with the source of every conclusion. The decision stays with your team.

        How much does it cost?

        The pilot is free. We're at MVP stage: before talking about paid plans, we want the product to fit your programme.

        What happens to the startups when the programme ends?

        They can keep using Ozmetra with a rollout tailored to them, because it's a hybrid SaaS we adapt to each company. That way, what's worked on in the programme carries on day to day.

        Can we see it before deciding?

        Of course. We'll show you by video call or in person at your premises. Leave your phone number or request the pilot and we'll arrange it. Book a call

        Does Ozmetra certify startups for ISO?

        No. We help them prepare the processes, documentation and evidence that standards such as ISO 9001 or ISO/IEC 20000-1 require. Certification is granted by an accredited body after its audit.

        What is your method based on?

        On the LightSME and LightStartup frameworks, published in a scientific journal and conference, based on the ISO/IEC 33000 family and validated with real SME and startup cases.
